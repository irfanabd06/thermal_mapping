title: Introduction
---
Greenhouse cultivation offers a protective environment for crops against harsh weather and pests. However, in tropical regions such as Singapore, controlling temperature and humidity can pose significant challenges. Cooling system performance depends heavily on design and placement. Optimising this alongside installation and operational costs is crucial to maintain profitability for farmers.

To achieve that, farmers will have to understand the thermal behaviours of their own greenhouses. This knowledge allows them to make informed decisions regarding design and cooling systems, leading to strategic planning for climate control and resulting in healthier crops and operational sustainability.
