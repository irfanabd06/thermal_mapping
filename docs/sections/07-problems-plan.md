### 7.3. Scope of Work

This project covers one part of GH1: the 20 m-long germination section, used as the test section. All sensing, mapping, simulation and fan tests take place there, while the full greenhouse is the long-term application (Table 16). Only fans are varied; the greenhouse structure stays fixed.

[[table: table-scope | Table 16: Scope of work]]

**Out of scope:** changes to GH1's structure, vents, misting or shading; guaranteeing crop-optimal temperatures at peak sun, proving yield or profit improvement.
