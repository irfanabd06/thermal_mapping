How the map is built

  • The 57 readings are placed on a grid of 19 columns along the house and 3 rows across it, all at 1 m height. Spacing was 3 to 3.5 m on site. The map uses 3.1 m along the house and 3.25 m across, so any point may be up to about 0.5 m from where it was taken.
  • Colour between points is a smooth interpolation of the readings. The strips between the outer rows and the walls, and beyond the first and last columns, repeat the nearest reading.
  • Only one height was measured, so the map says nothing yet about temperature near the roof or at floor level.
  • The readings carry no time stamps. Outdoor temperature and sun changed while the survey was walked, so part of the pattern may be time, not place.

Model basis

  • Confirmed: 3.00 m walls, 3.35 m main ridge, and a raised ridge section about 3 m wide that runs the full length and tops out at 4.35 m. Target 29 °C.
  • Confirmed: walls are 0.3 mm insect netting on the lower half and 0.3 mm clear plastic with 0.5 mm shading over it on the upper half. The roof is the same plastic and shading.
  • Confirmed: the hump is a floor hump left from the old road, 2.1 m wide and 150 mm high at its peak, about 20 m from the front wall.
  • Plan built from the labelled wall lengths: 36 m straight section, a transition of 3.67 m and 2.48 m, then a wing of 19.53 m and 20.5 m. These give a 5.5° bend and about 59 m along the centreline.
  • Not yet known: which compass direction the house faces. Wind direction and sun bearing cannot be tied to a wall until it is.
  • Still assumed: the fan in the wing marked 9 on the layout is fan 7. Bed widths, tank sizes, station sizes and fan heights are scaled from the drawing and photos.
